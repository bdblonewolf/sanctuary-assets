**Copyright rule.** Every file is original synthesis or a verified CC0 recording (licence and source URL logged per file). Diablo titles are a feel reference only: no ripped, sampled or re-recorded Blizzard audio, and no "sound-alike" copies of a specific cue.

## 3. How the lists were merged (dedupe rules)
- Footsteps: one surface set (Eng P0-09/P1-01, Env §8, Chr class kit) plus a 3-tier armour overlay (light, mail, plate) played on top, instead of a 3 tiers x 9 surfaces matrix. Heavy foe steps (Eng P1-02) live in the S-BRUTE, S-BEAR, S-PLATE and S-MAW sets.
- Containers: Eng P0-19/P0-20/P1-25 and Env loot_low props are one row per sound type (one open sound per container type). hollow_log joins crate/log; rock_pile joins rubble_cache; remains_heap joins body search; desert pots join burial urns.
- Hits: one impact set by material (flesh, bone, heavy, plate, ghost; Eng P0-11) replaces per-monster hit sounds. Monster sets add only the vocal (hurt grunt, squeal, chip).
- Deaths and vocals: Eng P0-15 (death per family), P0-16 (hurt/attack per family) and P1-16 (aggro) map onto the 16 Chr shared sets. Per-model rows carry only the extra layer.
- Elements: Eng P0-17 (rite impact by damage type) and the Chr element layers are one set of stems (E-PHYS, FIRE, COLD, LIGHT, POIS), reused by monsters, bosses, barrels and casks. Elite aura loops (Eng P1-15) and the Env elite/boss area swell are one loop set.
- Spells stay code-generated: the 51 rite cues, traps and auras get no files. The engine's proposed rite school file layers (P1-12) are held as an open question.
- Fires and lights: town torches, crypt sconces and church candles share one small-flame loop. The bonfire loop covers the town pyre and camp bonfires.
- Books: one page-turn set covers folios, signs, lecterns, Quill's foley and tome loot; one quill-scratch set covers Gravewell's Ledger tick, Quill and the Tallyman.
- Coins: one gold-spill set covers gold drops, Purse-Jaw's coin spray and the toll bosses' coin spills.
- Portals: the hell-gate portal sounds also serve the Town Portal Scroll (Eng P1-09).
- Potions: the drink sound replaces the per-class potion gulp; the player kit's level-up exhale is a P2 layer on the level-up sound.
- Boss telegraphs: the four code kinds (generic, summon, target, phase) are P0; the 12 attack-shape wind-ups are P1 and shared by every boss. Per-boss rows carry only signature sounds.

**Priority rules.** Gameplay feedback keeps the engine's priority. Where two lists disagree on the same sound, the more urgent one wins, except ambience beds outside Martsirt, which follow the engine (the environment list's P0 means the art is built, not that play needs it). Monster families that already exist in the live game get P0 for their attack, hurt and death core even when their models are P1.

## 5. Open questions for Brian
1. **Quill's voice: RESOLVED (Brian, 2026-11-01).** Archaic and precise, low near-whisper with a conspirator's smile, small pauses before names, dry chuckle, sniff or glance-away breath when deflecting. Furtive but warm, never menacing. See /workspace/sanctuary_art/lore/quill_martsirt.md.
2. **Class genders.** No class has a set gender, so PL-01..03 and PL-06..08 plan male and female sets (64 P0 files). Keep both, or fix one sex per class and drop 32 P0 files?
3. **Voices under the copyright rule.** CC0 libraries have wordless efforts and breaths but almost no scripted lines in the right register. Wordless vocals work as CC0 + processing. For NPC barks with words (480 files), may we record an original performer with full ownership, or should barks stay wordless (grunts plus on-screen text)? Also: what is the source and licence of the existing voice/{male,female} greeting samples?
4. **Creature ambiguities.** Vesper Caller's element is guessed as cold from the blue rune glow. Gibbet Remains' attack (a cage ram) is a guess. Wolf Howl wolves have no design (S-WOLF planned at P1). Spawn Shaman is P1 but the P0 Spawnling's flee squeal and revive depend on it. Tallyman and Ghel are P1 per bosses.json but their zones sit on the Act III coast road. Bilge-Wight's act is unknown (treated as P1). Corrupted Phimn has no zone (P1). Bram is in Martsirt or Briarwood. Ayla has no voice entry. Female greeting samples are missing for Myrine, Iskra and Moth. Diablo equivalents for the existing and Margins bosses are guesses.
5. **Codec: Vorbis vs Opus.** All three lists and the world brief ask for Ogg Vorbis; nobody asked for Opus. The engine adds AAC .m4a twins for iOS Safari (a failed Ogg decode is silent). Plan: Vorbis q4 plus m4a twins, no Opus. Confirm?
6. **Mono vs stereo for ambient beds.** The delivery spec says 44.1 kHz mono one-shots. The engine and the world brief want stereo beds and stingers; the environment list only says OGG. Plan: mono for all one-shots and positional loops (bonfire, torches, creek, chains, auras), stereo for non-positional beds (wind, crypt tone, coast swell, rain) and boss/death stingers. Confirm?
7. **Sample rate and loudness.** The engine allows 22.05 kHz for beds and footsteps; the world brief calls 22.05 kHz files placeholders. Plan: 44.1 kHz everywhere. The spec peak is -1 dBTP; the world brief asks for SFX peaks at -6 dBFS and beds at -8 dBTP to leave room for the A1 limiter. Which ceiling wins?
8. **File naming.** The engine spec (audio/sfx/<group>/<name>_<nn>.ogg) is used for every row. Characters suggested sfx/mon/, sfx/boss/<id>/ and sfx/pc/<class>/<sex>/, and the engine also proposed audio/amb/<zone>.ogg for beds. NPC barks stay under the existing audio/voice/<sex>/ path because greet() loads from there. OK?
9. **Priority conflicts.** The environment list marks forest, coast and crypt beds, the longship and 6 extra footstep surfaces as P0; the engine has them at P1/P2. They are P1 here (Martsirt beds stay P0). Bonfire: engine P2, environment P0, set to P0. Thunder: P2 vs P1, set to P1. Critters: P2 vs P1, set to P1. Block: engine P1 vs Footman block P0, set to P1 (synth ching stays as the fallback). Loot tiers: engine 3 groups vs one sound per tier, set to 5 tiers.
10. **Rite school file layers.** The engine proposes 20 file layers under the synth rite casts (P1-12), but the rule is that spells stay code-generated. Held as code with no files. Same for traps (Ironjaw is a physical bear trap) and aura beds. Allow any of these as files?
11. **Variants and loop lengths.** Characters ask for at least 3 hit and death variants and 4 to 6 footsteps; the world brief says 2 to 3 deaths; the engine wants 6 footsteps for P0 surfaces and 4 for P1. Used 3+ and 6/4. Beds are 60 s loops (fires 20 to 30 s); creature loops are 4 to 8 s.
