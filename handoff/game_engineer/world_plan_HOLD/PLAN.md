## Scale ("half of Skyrim")
- **Footprint:** 4.5 x 4.5 km = **20.25 km²**.
- **Land:** ~11.3 km². The rest is the SW sea and the Blackwater channel.
- **Walkable now:** 0.065 km² (23 outdoor zones on their live grids; coast at 42x112).
- **Walkable later:** **2.386 km²** (36 zones: the 23 existing ones grown, plus 13 NEW zones on HOLD). That is 12 % of the footprint and 21 % of the land.
- **Stretch:** taking every zone to the 400x400 cap would give ~5.8 km² walkable. It isn't recommended for phones: border kit tris scale with perimeter, and nav scales with area.

## Illusion tricks (how 20 km² reads from 2.4 km² walkable)
1. **Massifs and water as dividers.** Each chain is walled by impassable mountain massifs (mountain border style plus impostor ridges, no collision, no nav). The SW sea splits the coast from Flotsam/Hollow, and Blackwater stays an island reached by boat only. The outer world edge uses the coast and mountain border styles only.
2. **Roads as the skeleton.** Every zone is a road corridor with plateaus off it. A seam is always a road, exits are offset, and the neighbour's scenery is copied into the margin.
3. **Repeated kit.** The same 6 border styles × 4 seasons rebuild the ring for every zone (standing rule). Villages reuse Martsirt buildings.
4. **Zones ≤ ~400 m**, fog (town 30/110; ring fog near = 16 + (D − 14)), and procedural filler (instanced rocks and trees) on massif faces.

## Geography
- **Martsirt is the hub**, flat at 0 m.
  - S gate → Ashen Coast (0 m, sea W/S) → the coast road climbs east through Bastion (switchbacks) → Mourning Bluffs → Silt Mires → Razor Ridge → Bitter Ascent → Iron Bastion Pass → Inquisitor's Gaol → Obsidian Spires (72 m summit plateau).
  - Side branches: Sunken Crypts → Drowned Chasm; the Putrid Sink drops below the Mires.
  - N gate → Briarwood (6 m) → **the west road**: Briarwood East (12 m ridge) → **The Narrow** (pass, drops 6 → 1.5 m) → **desert basin** (1.5 m) → Qadren → Highmarket → Cinder Barrens.
- **Boat links:** Ashen Coast ↔ Bleak Flotsam (bay) → Smuggler's Hollow; Flotsam/Coast ↔ Blackwater (island).
