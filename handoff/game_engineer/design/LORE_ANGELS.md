# Sanctuary Lore: The Angels (stored 2026-11-01, Brian)

Status: lore reference only. Not for implementation yet.
Story beat (Brian, 2026-11-01): the hero meets the first angel shortly after defeating the first main boss.

## Brian's core idea
- Angels are bound NOT to intervene.
- They look on man's corruption with frustration and judge that man has forsaken himself.
- Their tone is that of stern, scolding teachers: disappointed, never comforting, yet still leaving the choice to man.

## Brian's original lines (reference)
> "You fools ought cast a stone, evil now bekins this forsaken swamp of self servitude."
> "Brace thyself. The dark wood grows. Eaten or condemned, once the reaper sows, the tighter the threads fit. It is up to thee, if not too late."

## Archaic renderings
1. "Fools! Ye that would not cast a stone 'gainst it, behold: evil now beckoneth thee into this forsaken mire of thine own self-servitude."
2. "Brace thyself, child of dust. The dark wood groweth. Devoured or damned, once the Reaper hath sown, the tighter do the threads draw. 'Tis left unto thee... if it be not already too late."

## Expanded lines (same voice)
- "We were forbidden the sword, and ye were given it. Wherefore then doth it rust in thy scabbard?"
- "Thou criest unto Heaven as a babe unto its mother. Heaven heareth. Heaven doth not stoop."
- "Each soul ye sell for coin is a thread the Weaver taketh up. Count thy threads, mortal, ere they bind thee."
- "Thy fathers built their altars high and their hearts low. Now the rot climbeth what they raised."
- "I am not thy shepherd. I am the witness that shall speak of thee when the fire cometh."
- "Look not upon me for mercy. Look upon thy hands; there it was laid, and there it was squandered."
- "The wood remembereth every oath ye broke beneath its boughs. It hungereth for the oath-breakers first."
- "Rise, or be counted among the fallen. Both are of thy choosing, and neither of mine."
- (parting) "Go, then. Prove me wrong, if thou canst. I have waited long to be wrong."

## Hooks
- The Briarwood angel bust statue (map wild) could be the first sign of this order: a watcher carved, not a saviour.
- The "threads" and "Reaper sows" motifs could tie into the dark-wood corruption spreading map by map.
