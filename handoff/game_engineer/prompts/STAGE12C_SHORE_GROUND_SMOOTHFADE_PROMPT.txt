STAGE 12C - Smooth occlusion fade, splat outdoor ground, sand shore band, rounded map corners

Goal: (A) Replace the FIX1 Bayer dither (visible 4x4 pixel grid) with a real alpha fade: each faded dress instance is drawn by a second, transparent InstancedMesh, so a tree between the camera and the hero becomes about 38% opaque, with no pixel grid. (B) Replace the dungeon kit floor tiles on outdoor maps (yE zones: not Martsirt, not dungeons, churches or town interiors) with one splat-blended ground mesh per zone, textured by season, with soft noise-broken transitions. Where there is sea, add a dry/wet sand band along the coast, shelve the ground under the water instead of ending on a square 0.4 m step, and round the map corners to match the backdrop ring lip. Kit beaches and cliffs are stage 12D. 3D only.
