SANCTUARY 3D — STAGE 1 of 5: one lit 3D dungeon room, hero walking, camera rotate/zoom.

RULES FOR THIS RUN (budget is limited)
- Do ONLY stage 1. Do not touch skills, items, auras, drops, quests, UI menus or balance.
- Work in small steps. If a step fails twice, STOP and report. Do not retry in loops.
- Keep the current 2D game fully working. Add the 3D view behind a toggle: Settings > "3D View (beta)", default OFF. Turning it off must give the exact current game.

GOAL
The target look is Diablo 2 remade in 3D: dark, gritty, torchlit stone. It is not cartoon.

1. ENGINE
- Add Three.js (npm "three"). Create a separate renderer module, e.g. src/render3d/.
- Game logic, positions and combat stay where they are. The 3D view only READS the existing world state (hero position, facing, moving/idle) each frame.
- Map 1 existing world unit to 1 3D unit on the ground plane (x, z).

2. CAMERA
- Isometric-style view: perspective camera, about 50° downward tilt, looking at the hero, smooth follow.
- Two-finger twist (or Q/E on desktop) rotates 360° around the hero.
- Pinch (or mouse wheel) zooms from far, a wide isometric view, to close, a near third-person feel. Clamp both ends.
- Movement input must stay relative to the screen after rotating: tapping a spot still walks there. Raycast taps onto the ground plane.

3. ONE TEST ROOM
About 30x30 units, built from simple boxes and cylinders (no external models yet):
- A cracked cobblestone floor (procedural canvas texture plus normal map, dark grey-brown).
- Stone brick walls about 4 units tall.
- Two pillars, a short stair up to a raised platform, and one sunken pit.
- Two dark red banners on the walls.
- All materials are MeshStandardMaterial, low roughness variation, desaturated colors.

4. LIGHTING (the most important part)
- Very low ambient light. Most of the room sits in near-black shadow.
- One pale blue "moonlight shaft" (a spotlight from above on the room center) plus a faint visible cone (an additive transparent mesh).
- Four warm orange wall torches, point lights with a slight flicker. Only the shaft light and at most 1 torch cast real-time shadows. The rest don't cast shadows.
- Add light fog that fades distance to black.
- Use ACES tone mapping and sRGB output.

5. HERO PLACEHOLDER
- A simple armored knight from primitives: body, helmet, sword and shield, grey steel metal material.
- It faces the move direction and has a simple walk bob while moving.
- It casts a shadow.

6. PHONE PERFORMANCE
- Cap pixel ratio at 1.5. Shadow map 1024. Merge static geometry.
- Target 60 fps, minimum 30 fps on a mid-range Android.
- Show a small fps counter only when the 3D toggle is on.

DONE WHEN
- The toggle switches between the old 2D view and the new 3D room.
- The hero walks around the 3D room by tapping.
- Rotate and zoom work by touch.
- The room looks dark with pools of blue and orange light.
- The 2D game is unchanged with the toggle off.

When done, report:
- the files changed
- the fps you measured or estimated
- anything you skipped and why
Then stop.
