1. **Mechanic** = the `desc` column, value v (shown "pp→cap" = per point → cap), kind as in section E (HIT/BOLT/DOT/AREA/DUR/SPEED/SLOW/DEBUFF/CRIT/CHANCE/HEAL/DEF/OTHER), applied on your basic attack hits (and swings) while that rite is Bound. %RD = % of the bound rite's average hit. Only the row's own effect; `boundMul` damage multiplier is removed (Bound keeps the +20% AR).

3. **Tradeoff**: `IAS −N raw` = subtract N from raw IAS before diminishing returns; `dmg ×N` = multiply that weapon's basic hit damage; `block −N%`, `FRW −N%`, `proj spd ×N` apply while Bound; `RULE:` = implement the rule exactly as written.
4. **Status** (column st), applied on the mechanic's hit: chance 20% (stun/freeze/fear 10%); stun 0.6s; freeze 1s; fear 1.5s; root 1s; chill 3s (−30% speed); slow 2s (−25% speed); poison 3s at 10%·RD/s; ignite 3s at 10%·RD/s; bleed 4s at 8%·RD/s; shock 3s (+1 existing `shockN`, max 10); weaken 3s (−15% damage dealt); blind 2s (50% miss); knockback 1m push; pull 1.5m `dragToward`; crit = that hit always crits; burning_ground = 2s patch at 10%·RD/s. Bosses half durations; elites/bosses immune to fear.
5. **Tooltip** (Bound row in the rite tooltip and the Bound menu): `Bound ({weapon}): {desc}. {v:.1f}{unit} (cap {cap}). At {bonusRank}: {bonus}. Cost: {tradeoff}.` Status shown as `{chance}% to {Status} for {dur}s`.
