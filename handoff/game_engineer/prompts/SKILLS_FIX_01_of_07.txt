## B3. Attack Rating skills use your attack damage (Diablo 2 rule)
Rule (Brian): physical/weapon skills that roll Attack Rating add to your attack damage; they are not separate damage. Spells, traps and auras are unchanged.

**What the live bundle does now** (routes-C8uY1u6h.js, re-fetched 2026-10-27, unchanged):
- Basic attack (`heroSwing` / `looseHero` / `looseWand`): `strikeRoll()` rolls the weapon min-max of `player.atk`, where `atk = (weapon avg + added flat + passive flat + Whetstone) × atkM × (1+Enhanced Damage) × passive Damage%` (stat recalc, `be = …`). Then ×(1+0.03×Brawn) (bow: Mark), ×(1+0.01×Fortune), ×`boundMul`, Riposte, Wolf, full crit (×1.75), Double, Execute/exec, Berserk, cold conversion, fire brand, crippling. Rolls AR with `boundBonus`.
- Every rite (`castRite` → `mt()` → `pt()` → `Qe()`): damage = **average only, no min-max roll** = (weaponShare × (weapon × (1+ED) × (1+passive Damage%) + Whetstone + flat) + (1−weaponShare) × level spell base `Xe(lv)`) × `mul×(0.7+0.045(r−1))` × (1+Ce(stat)) × Fortune 0.5%/pt × school% × synergy. weaponShare `qe()` = 1 for Wide Cut, Blade Dance, Execute, Rupture, Blind Shield Rush, Javelin, Aimed Shot, Smite; 0.5 Blood Soaked; 0 all others. Stat uses diminishing `Ce()` (30 → +60%, 90 → +108%, cap +180%) instead of the sheet's linear 3%/pt; crit is half chance; no Execute/Berserk/Double/cold conversion/fire brand. AR roll only if `A[id].usesAR` (7 rites), bonus `x(rank)` = 25%+3%/rank (cap 100%).
