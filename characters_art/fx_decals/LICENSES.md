All decal textures (textures/*), meshes (glb/*) and scripts (src/*) are **original procedural work** generated for Sanctuary by src/gen_fx_decals.py / src/build_glb.py (metaball blobs, droplets, drip trails, fbm noise edges, Voronoi lava cracks). No third-party images were used in them. Licence: CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Reuse check: existing town decal atlases (models/town/*/materials/*/m_decal_atlas_*) and sanctuary_env gen_decals_v4.py contain only mud/leaf-litter decals, no blood/goo; the blob()/fbm noise approach from gen_decals_v4.py was adapted (our own code).

Free CC0 sources were reviewed (ambientCG, Poly Haven, OpenGameArt CC0, Kenney); no suitable CC0 512px alpha blood/goo/lava decals were adopted, so all were generated procedurally.
