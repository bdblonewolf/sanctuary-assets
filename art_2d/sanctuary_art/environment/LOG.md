# Sanctuary environment concept sheets: log

- `01a_qadren_establishing.jpg`: Qadren (the Act II town after Martsirt, per the story bible). Dusk isometric view of the small walled sandstone and mud-brick town: free well plaza, domed Dry Reliquary with stairs down, watchtower, black-glass dunes. Three vignettes and a palette strip.
- `01b_qadren_building_fronts.jpg`: Eight Qadren front elevations: Tamsin's kitchen-inn, Orlem's house, Vetch's dice house, Heska's watch post, free well house, Dry Reliquary, caravanserai stable, coppersmith/trader.
- `02a_desert_city_silhouettes_materials.jpg`: Large walled desert city (working title, no name yet): skyline panorama with an aqueduct toward the Qadren road, 5 silhouette studies (gatehouse, citadel, great dome, tower houses, bell tower), and 8 material swatches (sandstone, lime plaster, mud-brick, render, striped awning, canvas, cedar, rusted iron).
- `02b_desert_city_signature_buildings.jpg`: Six signature buildings for the desert city: Great Gate, Citadel, Domed Cistern Temple, Covered Bazaar, Caravanserai & Customs House, Clock-Mage Observatory (ruin).
- `03a_castle_kit_walls_gates.jpg`: Castle kit. Curtain wall (straight and breached), round and square towers, gatehouse, spiked portcullis, spiked gate, iron grille door, carved archway door, and a curved fan staircase on a cobbled courtyard. A large panel shows a pair of guardian-knight statues holding lanterns beside a dungeon portcullis, with a stair going down. Refs were used for mood only.
- `03b_gothic_kit_ironwork_glass.jpg`: Gothic kit. Six rusted ironwork pieces (wall hook, chain hook, strap hinge, torch sconce, lantern bracket, spiked railing), four stained-glass windows (rose, saint, grisaille, broken), three church walls (nave wall, flying buttress, ruined chapel wall), and a white-marble balcony with a winged angel statue.
- `04_material_swatches.jpg`: Twelve labeled floor and wall swatches under shared moonlit lighting, from filthy to holy: cellar concrete, mud floor, earth and straw, tavern planks, rubble wall, mud-brick, cobblestone courtyard, crypt flagstones, castle ashlar, encaustic tiles, dark veined marble, heavenly white marble.
- `05_hellgate_statues.jpg`: Four hell-gate statues over crypt-stair portals, in the weeping-angel style: A Mourner (hooded and weeping), B Sentinel Angel (wings and sword), C Black Monolith (chained and inscribed), D Veiled Pietà.
- `06_qadren_layout_map.jpg`: Aged-parchment top-down Qadren layout: walled compact town, West Gate (from Martsirt), East Gate road to the walled desert city, central Free Well plaza, all 8 buildings + Dry Reliquary (stairs down), watchtower, grain store, sealed north postern; labels partly PIL-patched.
- `07a_qadren_interiors_inn_coppersmith.jpg`: Cutaway interiors: Tamsin's Kitchen-Inn (hearth, dome oven, soup cauldron, tables, rugs) and the Coppersmith & Trader (copper shelves, pierced lanterns, anvil, forge).
- `07b_dry_reliquary_interior.jpg`: Dry Reliquary: torchlit stair plunging down + first crypt chamber (saint niches, ossuary chests, altar with empty opened reliquary, toppled effigies, frost); no gore; garbled wall inscriptions blurred to worn stone.
- `08_qadren_clutter.jpg`: 16 labeled props: awnings, canvas shade, pierced brass lamp, iron cage lantern, water jar, copper pot, rugs, kettle/ewer/dice shop signs, caravan pack saddle, waterskins & rope, handcart, dead date palm, desert scrub (saddle & palm cells PIL-patched).
