# Quill, Tome Keeper of Martsirt (lore log)

**Where:** the southwest corner of Martsirt square, beside the coffer he will not lock. Inside it lies the flesh-bound tome, and its warmth is not a metaphor; he has been burning his sleeves on it.

**Personality:** Soft-spoken, precise, faintly amused. He answers questions fully, then watches thee realize the answer does not help. He is not bitter; he is consistent. He will praise genuine skill when he sees it, then point out at once that skill changes nothing about the choice he has already made.

**Core burden (woven into him, not lectured):**
Quill once kept a high seat in the iron citadel, among the knights who answer no horn now. He knew its halls. He left because the man who could remain there was a stranger he would have had to keep performing. The corner asks no performance. Over the years the preference hardened into who he is. Even now, when he can name every reason the high seats would be better, going back feels like erasing the only self he still recognizes. He calls this "keeping the shape." The tome suits him: a thing that stays warm in a box no one locks, and is never carried anywhere.

**Sample dialogue (archaic tone):**
- "They still speak my name behind the citadel gates, when the wind comes off the barges. High seats. Warm light. Men who would have followed me into the dark, had I only stayed and named the path. I remember the weight of that light. It was not heavy. That is the part they never believe."
- "Thou askest why I remain. Because the man who could walk back up those steps is a man I have not been for years. To become him again, I would first have to kill the one who sits in this corner. I have grown... accustomed to his company."
- "Tell me I cannot reach the Coast and back before the tide turns, and I will find a way. Tell me the far gate is already mine, and I will sit here until the square forgets my weight. The difference is not courage. It is recognition."
- "They stopped testing me once they decided I could do anything. A kindness, they called it. I have starved on that kindness ever since."
- "The square pretends it is a world. I do not fault it. I pretend this corner is enough."

**If the player has just done something hard:**
- "Thou carried it. Good. Now set it down before it learns thy name. Some burdens only stay light while thou pretendest they were never thine to keep."

**Design note:** He offers no quest that "fixes" him. His tome and Boss Stone duties stay as they are. Beyond them he gives accurate descriptions of the trap, then goes back to watching the square and the warm coffer. Players who keep talking to him slowly realize he is not stuck. He chooses, every day, with full knowledge, and the knowledge has not been enough.
