# Sanctuary art log: monsters

## Global monster rule
- A monster's skin/body hue ALWAYS shifts toward its main damage element: fire = red, ice = blue, lightning = pale violet-white, poison = sickly green (etc.).

## 2026-10-31: Corrupted Phimn, LOCKED IN by Brian
- Lore: demon-corrupted Phimn (the near-extinct grey-blue race; see ../races/LOG.md and Ayla). Inspired by Diablo 2's corrupted spear-woman rogue and the succubus; all names and designs are original.
- Form A, Winged Caster (corrupted_phimn_caster.jpg): horns, glowing eyes, torn bat wings; hovers; casts elemental magic; blackened leather/bone succubus-style gear; red runes spread with the corruption.
- Form B, Feral Stalker (corrupted_phimn_feral.jpg): no wings; runs on all fours, smells blood, ambushes with long demon claws; fangs; bone spear on her back.
- Each form is shown in fire / ice / lightning / poison hue variants.
- Base: the Phimn 4'8" frame (the feral concept reads heavier; keep the build close to the Phimn frame when modeling).
